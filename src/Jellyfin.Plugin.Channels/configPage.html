Channels

A channel runs on the wall clock whether or not anyone is watching. Tuning in joins whatever is airing rather than resuming where you left off.

Channel

What the channel is, in a line. Describes the channel itself, not whatever happens to be airing.
Decides what "6:00 AM" means for this channel. Stored with the channel and never taken from the server, so a wrong server clock cannot quietly shift the schedule.

Dayparts

Split the broadcast day if you want different content at different times. One daypart covering 00:00–24:00 is an ordinary channel. Gaps are allowed and mean off air.
